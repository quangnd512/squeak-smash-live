How to play

  • ⏱️Each round lasts 60 seconds. The mice pop up faster as the round goes on.
  • 🐭Tap a mouse to whack it for 1 point. A golden mouse is worth 5. A hard-hat mouse takes 2 hits and is worth 3.
  • 🐰Don't whack Clover the bunny! She shows up more and more as the round goes on. You lose 1 point and are stunned for 1 second.
  • 🔥Whack 3 mice in a row and get 2 bonus points. The meter under Combo shows how close you are. Missing breaks your streak.
  • 🏳️Every round counts. Your score is added to your country's total, with no limit on how often you play. Country totals reset on the 1st of every month.

No account needed. Your player lives in this browser, and only your name and country are shown to others. Please pick a friendly name.
